CASE STUDIES

Real Work. Real Result

Men's Clothing

• Paid Media • Meta & Google

THE CHALLENGE

The brand had a strong product but almost no online sales. Their store wasn't converting, paid campaigns burned budget on irrelevant traffic, customer acquisition costs kept climbing, and ad fatigue set in fast. Revenue was flat and every new order cost more than the last. In one of the most competitive fashion markets in the world, "more spend" wasn't the answer.

THE SOLUTION

I rebuilt the entire acquisition journey from the ground up. Sharper audience targeting replaced spray-and-pray reach. A high-velocity creative testing framework, lifestyle hooks against product hooks, kept winning ads fresh and drove cost-per-result down. Multi-step landing funnels and post-purchase retention flows turned one-time buyers into repeat customers. Every rupee was measured against one number: profitable, blended ROAS.

The result: A struggling store became a consistent revenue engine, scaling to a 4.96x ROAS on the main campaign and bringing in steady, profitable sales month after month.

These shifts translated into measurable improvements:

4.96x ROAS on core campaign
₹28.7L Purchase value generated
3,353 Website purchases driven
₹855 Average order value

Wellness Brand

• Paid Media • Meta & Google • Organic social Media

THE CHALLENGE

A premium, high-ticket product in a category most people had never heard of. Low awareness of PEMF therapy made it hard to build trust, and even harder to sell expensive devices online — to both everyday consumers and B2B buyers.

THE SOLUTION

I engineered a full-funnel system where organic and paid worked as one. Education-first content built authority and warmed a cold market, while precision Meta & Google campaigns captured that demand at the exact moment of intent. Two audiences, two buying journeys, one machine — D2C conversions and B2B enquiries pulled from the same funnel, every rupee measured against profitable return.

The results: A lesser-known category became a demand-generating brand — a reliable engine producing high-quality B2B leads while driving consistent, profitable online sales of premium products, month after month.

These shifts translated into measurable improvements:

4.25x ROAS — AED 4.25 back for every AED 1 spent
68% Lower cost per acquisition after optimisation
AED 6.8L Online sales driven, D2C
-41% Drop in cost-per-lead over the campaign

Real Estate Company

• Paid Media • Meta

THE CHALLENGE

Inconsistent lead flow and poor lead quality. The team couldn't rely on a steady pipeline, and the leads that did come in rarely converted — making it impossible to plan sales or forecast growth.

THE SOLUTION

I rebuilt their lead-generation system from the ground up. Tighter targeting, stronger offers, and qualification built directly into the funnel — so the campaigns attracted serious, ready-to-act buyers instead of cheap clicks. Every lead was engineered to reach the sales team already warm.

The Result: The pipeline was consistently full of quality leads at a healthy CPL, and the site-visit booking rate climbed, turning leads into real sales conversations, not dead ends.

These shifts translated into measurable improvements:

₹10L+ Ad spend managed & optimised
-58% Reduction in cost per lead
+23% Higher site-visit booking rate
